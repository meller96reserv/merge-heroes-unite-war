## Measurement limits

Source25fps imposes40ms frame granularity. Review sheets sampled10fps/5fps/2fps give coarser uncertainty explicitly reported in timing_measurements.csv. Command timestamps are captured after tool operations and have scheduling offset;never subtract them to claim exact animation length. No generalized balance curve/probability was fitted from this sparse sample. Offline3:34is displayed elapsed time,not a controlled test duration.
