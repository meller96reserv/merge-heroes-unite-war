# Privacy Policy — Merge Heroes Unite War

Updated: September 11, 2026

This policy describes information handled by Merge Heroes Unite War (package com.mergeheroes.unitewar) and its developer (“we”). It accompanies the linked Terms of Use. For support and privacy requests, use the developer contact details on the application's store listing or developer profile.

## Local game data
There is no account registration. Your progress, currency, heroes, equipment, reward receipts and sound/notification settings are saved locally on your device. We do not operate a game account or cloud-save backend. Clearing app data or uninstalling may erase these records.

## Analytics and technical information
The app integrates AppMetrica to understand launches, screen use, gameplay actions and technical problems. Depending on the platform and SDK configuration, this can involve app and operating-system versions, device model, language, SDK identifiers, IP-derived region, session timing and crash diagnostics. We do not send the full game save, message content or contact lists as custom analytics events. Precise location tracking is disabled. Analytics is initialized when you enter the game through Let’s Play.

AppMetrica is provided by Yandex. Its own privacy information and SDK documentation explain the information it processes: https://appmetrica.yandex.com and https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/.

## Optional rewarded advertising
Where configured and available, Start.io supplies video ads when you choose a reward button. We do not show automatic entry/return ads, banners or ordinary interstitials. The advertising SDK may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, device/app characteristics and ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Personalized advertising consent is not enabled by the game by default. Any device permissions or provider consent requirements still apply.

You may limit advertising identifiers or tracking through your operating-system settings. An unavailable or cancelled video does not grant a video reward. Start.io's privacy information is available at https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/.

## Notifications and support
Notifications are optional. Enabling them may request the operating system's permission; you can refuse or disable them in system settings. Reminders are scheduled locally and do not require supplying a phone number or email address.

If you contact support, your chosen contact identifier and the information you send are used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## Purchases and virtual items
The current game has no paid in-app purchases and does not collect card details. Virtual game items and currency have no monetary value and cannot be cashed out. This is a merge-and-battle game for entertainment, not a casino or real-money gambling service.

## Purposes, sharing and retention
Information is used to operate the game, diagnose faults, understand usage, deliver requested advertisements and answer support requests. SDK providers process information under their own policies and may do so in other countries. We do not operate a separate sale of user databases. Disclosures may also be required by applicable law.

Local saves remain until removed from the device or replaced by normal save updates. SDK and support records are kept according to the relevant provider settings and purposes, subject to applicable law; no single fixed retention period is promised here. Data security measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee that every system is completely secure.

## Your choices and rights
You can change sound and notification settings, control device permissions, clear local data or stop using the app. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to processing or withdraw consent. Submit requests through the developer contact channels shown on the application's store listing. For information held independently by an SDK provider, its privacy contact may also be relevant. We may need sufficient information to identify the affected records.

The game is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children. Contact support if you believe a child has supplied personal information so the issue can be investigated.

## External pages and updates
Legal pages and advertising links may open external services with their own privacy practices. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date. Review the current policy when the game's online features change.
